Moving enrollment to an online form saves lines, but only if the whole process is thought through. If families still have to go to the office to hand in a signed copy at the end, the form just added a step. These are the decisions worth settling before you open enrollment, in the order they usually come up.

## 1. Decide which documents you ask for and in what format

Make the list per grade, because preschool asks for different things than high school. At most Colombian schools the list includes:

- The student's civil birth registration (registro civil).
- The student's ID card (tarjeta de identidad), from age seven.
- Last year's report card or grade certificate, when the student comes from another school.
- A clearance letter (paz y salvo) from the previous school.
- Vaccination card, especially for preschool and primary.
- Proof of health insurance (EPS) enrollment.
- ID of the parents or guardian.

For each document, decide whether you accept a phone photo or need a PDF, and whether any original has to be handed in. If there are originals, say from the start when and where to bring them, so the family only goes once.

## 2. Publish spots and dates

Before opening the form, decide how many spots each grade has and the deadline for applications. If a grade fills up, the form should say so instead of letting a family fill everything in to find out later. Also publish the dates for interviews or admission tests, if you hold them.

## 3. Separate new enrollment from re-enrollment

A family that is already at the school should not have to type their address or their children's names again. Set up a short flow for returning students where they only confirm their data, update what changed (a phone number, the EPS, the guardian) and accept the new year's contract.

## 4. Decide who reviews and how long it takes

Every application needs an owner in the office. Agree on how many business days a review takes and state it on the form. Families accept waiting when they know for how long. Visible statuses such as "under review", "needs changes" and "confirmed" also help, so nobody has to call and ask.

## 5. Prepare the messages for missing or unclear documents

Some documents will be unreadable or incomplete. Write the messages for the common cases ahead of time, for example: "the photo of the birth registration is cut off, please upload it again with all four corners visible". That keeps answers clear and quick, whoever is reviewing that day.

## 6. Include the contract and the data authorization

Enrollment is also when families sign the education services contract and authorize the processing of personal data. Because this is data about minors, Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 requires the authorization to come from a parent or legal guardian, and the processing has to respect the student's best interest. If the form is online, acceptance can be online too: keep a record of who accepted and when.

## 7. Connect enrollment to the first payment

If the spot is confirmed when the enrollment fee is paid, the family should be able to see the amount and pay in the same place, by PSE or with the wallet they use. Splitting those two steps is the most common reason spots stay "pending" for weeks. Check that the amounts match the fees approved for your school in that year's cost resolution, and that the payment produces its electronic invoice.

## 8. Have the SIMAT information ready

Colombian schools report every student's enrollment in SIMAT, the Ministry of Education's enrollment system. If the form asks from the start for the data that report needs (the student's ID, date of birth, grade, school day schedule and campus), the office does not have to call families again in March to fill the gaps.

## 9. Test the form with three families

Before opening it to everyone, ask three families from different grades to fill it in on their phones. You will find confusing questions that nobody notices from the office: a field nobody knows how to fill, a document not everyone has at hand, a step that gets cut off on a small screen.

## A final check before opening

- The list of documents per grade is published and states the accepted format.
- Each grade shows its spots and deadline.
- Returning students have a short re-enrollment flow.
- The office knows who reviews each application and within how many days.
- The contract and the data authorization are accepted on the same form.
- The enrollment fee is paid in the same place and produces an invoice.
- The data SIMAT needs is captured from the start.
